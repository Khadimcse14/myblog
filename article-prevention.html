Everyday habits for preventive health

By MedGuide Health Editorial Team · 4 min read

PREVENTIVE CARE

Preventive health includes habits and appropriate healthcare that can support wellbeing and help identify potential problems early.

1. Keep up with appropriate checkups

Recommended preventive care varies based on age, personal history and risk factors.

2. Know your health information

Keeping track of medications, allergies and relevant health history can make healthcare conversations more useful.

3. Practice everyday hygiene

Basic hygiene habits, including regular handwashing and appropriate food safety, can support general health.

4. Discuss personal risks with a professional

A healthcare professional can help determine which preventive measures are appropriate for your individual circumstances.
