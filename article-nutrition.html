Understanding the basics of a balanced diet

By MedGuide Health Editorial Team · 4 min read

NUTRITION

A balanced eating pattern can provide a variety of nutrients that support everyday health and wellbeing.

1. Include a variety of foods

Different foods provide different nutrients. Including vegetables, fruits, whole grains and protein sources can help create a varied eating pattern.

2. Build balanced meals

Combining several food groups in a meal can help provide a broad range of nutrients. Individual needs and portions vary.

3. Stay hydrated

Water is an important part of everyday nutrition. Fluid needs vary with activity, climate and other factors.

4. Consider your individual needs

People with medical conditions or specific nutritional requirements may benefit from advice from a qualified healthcare professional or dietitian.
